## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance, profitability, discounts, country performance, product performance, and loss-making transactions using an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The objective is to transform raw financial data into actionable business insights that can support management decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability
- Compare financial performance across countries
- Identify the most and least profitable products
- Analyze profit margins
- Identify loss-making transactions
- Investigate major contributors to losses
- Provide recommendations for management

## 🗂 Dataset

- 701 rows
- 16 columns
- Sales data covering multiple countries, products and market segments

Key fields include:

`Country`, `Product`, `Segment`, `Sales`, `COGS`, `Profit`, `Discounts`, `Date`, and `Year`.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Country Performance

- The United States generated the highest sales at approximately 25M.
- France generated the highest total profit at 3.78M.
- Germany achieved the highest profit margin at 15.66%.
- The USA recorded the lowest profit margin at 11.97%.

### Product Performance

- Paseo generated the highest profit in the USA.
- Carretera was the least profitable product in the USA while remaining profitable.

### Loss Analysis

- Canada recorded the highest total loss at 164,488.75.
- VTT was a major contributor to Canada's identified losses.
- Three negative VTT transactions accounted for 77,393.75 of the identified loss.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the drivers behind Canada's loss-making transactions.
2. Review VTT's pricing, costs and discount levels in Canada.
3. Investigate why the USA generates the highest sales but the lowest profit margin.
4. Review the overall discount strategy and its effect on profitability.
5. Analyze Germany's high profit margin to identify practices that could be replicated in lower-margin markets.
